# Reference gaps {#gaps}

ATS-2025 section 2.1 and MTS-2023 section 2.1 say field work follows the latest edition of the codes they list, unless a later clause says otherwise. TestGuy’s Level II/III study threads add a shorter outside list (wiki.testguy.net/t/217 and /t/1391). This page is the documents those sources name that we cannot cite by clause.

A file in the private lookup folder is not the same as a usable copy. Several IEEE PDFs arrived as images or as notes, so they are listed here too.

Nothing on this page is cleared to commit. Official copies stay with the publisher.

## Usable for clause checks

These were read as text and may be cited.

- ANSI/NETA ATS-2025 and ANSI/NETA MTS-2023.
- NFPA 70E-2024.
- NFPA 110-2025. Emergency and standby power systems. Paraphrased notes from the free-access viewer; the notes stay private.
- 29 CFR 1910.147, 1910.269, and 1910.331 through 1910.335. OSHA 3120 is the booklet, not a substitute for the CFR.
- IEEE C57.104-2019 (and the 2008 edition, for history). Mineral-oil dissolved gas.
- IEEE 400.2-2024. VLF cable tests. ATS Table 100.6.3 says it is adapted from this edition. The tan-delta notes on ATS Table 100.6.7 still say they follow IEEE 400.2-2013 tables, and that 2013 edition is not here.
- IEEE 400.3-2022. Partial discharge on shielded cable.
- IEEE C57.106-2006. Oil acceptance. ATS Table 100.4’s note names IEEE C57.106-2015, which is not here, so 2015 clause numbers are not used.
- IEEE 519-2022. Harmonic limits. The file is a scanned copy; clause 5 and Table 1 were readable.
- IEEE C37.113-2015. Transmission-line relaying. Clause 3.2 adopts IEEE C37.2 device numbers but does not reprint the whole C37.2 list. Device names used in questions are the ones printed in ATS 7.9.1.
- IEEE C57.12.01-2015 and IEEE C57.12.10-2010 are in the folder. They were not the source of the questions in this batch.

## In the folder, not usable as the standard

| Document | What we actually have | Why it is still a gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IEEE C37.2 | Student notes, not the standard | Device-number questions use the names in ATS 7.9.1. C37.113-2015 clause 3.2 only confirms that the guide follows C37.2. |
| IEEE C57.91 | 1995 loading-guide corrigendum only | Not the loading guide. No loading questions are taken from it. |

Text-recognized copies, readable enough for clause checks, with OCR errors (megohm often prints as MQ):

- IEEE 43-2013. Insulation resistance and polarization index. Clause 12.2.2 is the 5000 megohm screen. Table 3 is the class PI floor. Table 4 is the 40 °C resistance floor.
- IEEE C57.13.1-2017. Field testing of relaying current transformers. Clauses 6, 8, 9, and 12.
- IEEE C37.91-2021. Transformer protection. Clauses 6.4.3, 6.5.2, 6.5.3, and 7.3.4.
- IEEE C57.155-2014. Dissolved gas in natural and synthetic esters. Clauses 4.1 and 5.3. Not a substitute for C57.104 on mineral oil.
