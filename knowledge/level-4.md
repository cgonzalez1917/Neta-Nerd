# Level IV expectations {#expectations}

*Level: IV*

Level IV is the senior certified technician: the person who can run the test, defend the number, and also judge a system. That means walking scheme logic, reading a coordination study against the as-left file, commissioning to ECS, and making a call on paralleling, CT application, or an ester-fluid sample without inventing a limit.

The December 2022 NETA detailed content outline, as summarized on the [test-day page](#/study/test-day.md/format), weights Level IV about:

- Safety — 10%
- Theory — 15%
- Component testing — 55%
- Systems and commissioning — 20%

Systems is a real share of the exam, not a leftover. Expect commissioning plans, function tests, phasing, power quality, and "what do you do first" troubleshooting, not only a bigger pile of component limits.

## Exam format {#exam-format}

*Level: IV*

Same published format as the other technician exams: closed book, Pearson VUE, on-screen calculator and formula sheet, scaled pass mark 410 on a 200 to 500 scale. Level IV is 65 questions in 2 hours, so there is a little more time per item than on Levels II and III, and more of those items ask you to choose a course of action. Details and the official formula sheet are on the [test-day](#/study/test-day.md/format) and [math](#/study/math.md/not-on-sheet) pages. Source: NETA’s technician-certification and exam-preparation pages, linked from the About page.
