# Level II expectations {#expectations}

Level II here means the NETA Assistant Electrical Testing Technician: routine tests with supervision still available, enough judgment to keep yourself safe, and enough procedure memory to set up a test and compare it with a table. The bank is weighted to this level.

The December 2022 NETA detailed content outline, as summarized by the TestGuy study guide, splits Level II roughly like this:

- Safety — 15%
- Electrical testing fundamentals / theory — 25%
- Component testing — 55%
- Systems and commissioning — 5%

People who already test gear often still miss Safety and Theory. Component familiarity does not carry those two. This bank follows that split on purpose.

## What Level II should be able to do

- Put a circuit in an electrically safe work condition, or say clearly why the task is still energized work.
- Pick a megohmmeter voltage, a DLRO, or a primary-injection set without guessing from the frame size alone.
- Know which NETA paragraph is a visual/mechanical inspection and which is an electrical test. The asterisk in ATS means optional.
- Correct insulation resistance for temperature before comparing it with a minimum.
- Compute power, power factor, turns-ratio error, and CT secondary current without being handed the rearranged formula.
- Read a one-line far enough to name the device, and a control schematic far enough to see a coil and a contact.

Level III adds interpretation, per-unit fault current, print logic, and the places where ATS and MTS disagree. Those pages are separate.

## Exam format {#exam-format}

This description comes from the TestGuy Level II study guide (wiki.testguy.net/t/217), which cites the NETA December 2022 detailed content outline. It is not a NETA publication and it can change.

- Closed book, delivered at Pearson VUE.
- About 100 questions in 2 hours.
- A NETA formula sheet is provided. Trig identities, the power triangle beyond `kW = kVA × PF`, copper temperature constants, insulation multipliers, and PI time points are not on that sheet. Drill those.
- An on-screen TI-30XS calculator is provided. Confirm degree mode before any cosine or angle.

Do not treat forum posts that recall specific items as the real exam. Write and study original questions about the same skills.
